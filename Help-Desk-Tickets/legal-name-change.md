# Help Desk Ticket: Legal Name Change
## Ticket Overview
**Request type:** Account Update - Legal Name Change
**Priority:** Low
**Department:** Finance
## Issue
A user submitted a request to update their account after a legal name change.
The request changes included:
-Update the user's display name
-Update the user's email address
-Keep previous email as an alias
-Maintain previous email continuity so the user does not miss messages
-Follow organization's approval process
## Resolution
The account update was completed according to the approved request. The user's display name and primary email information were updated while the previous email address was retained as an alias
## Skills Demonstrated
-User account management
-Email and alias management
-Following approval procedures
-Change Management
-Help desk documentation
-Attention to user communication continuity
## What I Learned
This ticket demonstrated the importance of following an organization's approval process when making account changes. Retaining the previous email address as an alias can also help prevent missed messages after an account update.
